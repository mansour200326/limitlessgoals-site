About us

Short version: a UAE general trading company that buys and sells commodities, and answers its own email.

Limitless Goals Import & Export Services is a general trading company based in the United Arab Emirates.

We trade wheat and grains, sugar, charcoal and other commodities. Our buyers and suppliers are across the Middle East and West and Central Africa.

We work both sides of a deal: we source from producers and exporters, and we supply importers, wholesalers and manufacturers. Every offer is built around the buyer's specification, quantity and destination, and terms are agreed in writing.

Enquiries are handled by our Commercial Director, Moaid Al Shafie.
