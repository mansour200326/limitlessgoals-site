Grains & wheat

Wheat and other grains for millers, food manufacturers, traders and distributors. We quote to your specification and destination, shipment by shipment.

Put this in your enquiry

  • Grain type and grade
  • Specification (protein, moisture, etc.)

Sugar

Charcoal

Charcoal for the trade, including coconut-shell charcoal briquettes for shisha (hookah), for the UAE and Egyptian markets. We work with producers on shapes, sizes and packing.

Put this in your enquiry

  • Charcoal type (e.g. coconut shell)
  • Shape and size (cube, flat, etc.)
  • Packing (inner box, carton)

  • Destination market
